# SERP deep dive: method, samples and cross-location findings (2026-11-05)

Locations covered: Lake Elmo, Woodbury, Chaska, Victoria, Minnetrista. One document per location follows this file (01 to 05). Everything below was captured from live Google results on November 5, 2026; nothing is inferred from brand mentions.

## How the capture was done

Two samples per query, as the brief requires, on the same day with different search contexts:

- Sample A (baseline): the Claude desktop browser, not signed in to Google, desktop layout, `hl=en gl=us`, 15 results requested. Google set the searcher location to the city in the query (shown as "Lake Elmo, MN", "Woodbury, MN" and so on under the results). Captured for Lake Elmo, Woodbury, Chaska and Victoria (44 queries). Google rate-limited this browser after query 45, so Minnetrista and the six statewide cost queries were captured in Sample B only.
- Sample B (second sample): Carson's own Chrome, signed in to Google. Personalization is visible: Schifsky pages rank 2 to 6 positions higher in this sample for several hire queries (for example "landscapers in Woodbury" is #7 in Sample A and #2 in Sample B). Treat Sample A as the ranking baseline and Sample B as the optimistic bound; where only Sample B exists (Minnetrista, statewide cost queries) the positions may be inflated by personalization.

For each query the capture recorded: the organic results in order with the ranking site, the local pack businesses with rating and review count and whether the entry was an ad, Local Services Ads when shown, the People Also Ask questions, whether an AI Overview appeared and which sources it listed, and the position of any schifskycompanies.com URL. Organic position counts only unpaid web results; Facebook, Yelp and Houzz listings are counted as results because they occupy slots.

Query patterns run per city (11): landscapers in {city}; landscaping company {city}; landscape design build {city}; landscape design {city}; paver patio {city}; retaining wall contractor {city}; pool installation {city}; outdoor kitchen {city}; pergola builder {city}; yard drainage {city}; how much does a paver patio cost in {city}. Statewide (7): retaining wall cost, outdoor kitchen cost, pergola cost, inground pool cost, plunge pool cost, fire pit cost, paver patio cost in Minnesota.

## Findings that hold across all five locations

1. Cost intent is won. Schifsky is #1 in Minnesota for retaining wall cost, outdoor kitchen cost, pergola cost (the service page), plunge pool cost (#1 and #2), fire pit vs fireplace cost, and paver patio cost (the concrete vs paver guide), and #2 for inground pool cost behind Plan Pools. The city patio cost guides are #1 for Lake Elmo and Woodbury; the concrete vs paver guide stands in for cities without a guide (Chaska #3, Victoria #2, Minnetrista #1). AI Overviews appear on every cost query and quote the $45 to $62 range on the Woodbury query.

2. Hire intent is won only where the page is a city page. The city hubs rank for "landscapers in {city}" and "landscape design build {city}" in every city that has one (Lake Elmo #2 design-build, Woodbury #4 to #7, Chaska #6, Victoria #4 to #9). They do not rank for the service-plus-city queries (paver patio, retaining wall, pool installation, outdoor kitchen) in Sample A. Competitors win those with city-specific service pages, and the pages are thin: 145 to 1,255 words, no prices, no named local projects (teardowns in each location file). Schifsky's service pages are metro pages that name each city once or twice, so they never appear; the city hubs mention each service under a generic heading ("Paver Patios and Hardscape") without the city name.

3. The wrong Schifsky page sometimes ranks. For "pergola builder {city}" the city hub ranks (Lake Elmo #5, Woodbury #9, Chaska #9, Victoria #3) instead of /aluminum-pergola-builder-minnesota/. For "paver patio Lake Elmo" the showroom page ranks #5 instead of the patio page or the Lake Elmo patio cost guide. For "outdoor kitchen Lake Elmo" The 45th project page ranks #6. These are relevance signals to fix with internal links and city mentions, not new pages.

4. The local pack is the biggest swing. Schifsky is in the Lake Elmo pack (positions 1 to 3) for landscapers, landscaping company, landscape design and design-build queries. It is in no other city's organic pack. Pioneer Landscaping LLC, also based in Lake Elmo, appears in the Woodbury pack for landscapers, landscaping company, design-build and drainage queries, so distance from Lake Elmo to Woodbury is not what keeps Schifsky out. Pioneer shows 102 reviews and the category "Landscaper"; Schifsky shows 78 reviews and the category "Landscape designer" on every pack entry. The Shakopee profile (2419 Eagle Creek Blvd, 13 reviews) appears only as a paid "Sponsored" pack entry in Minnetrista, Chaska and Victoria results; Mom's Design Build, based in Shakopee with 39 reviews, is in the Chaska organic pack, so the Shakopee profile can reach Chaska and Victoria packs once it has reviews and the right category.

5. Pools are a different SERP. "Pool installation {city}" is owned by pool builders (Valley Pools, Prestige, Plan Pools, Performance, Blaiser Pools) and the pack shows "Swimming pool contractor" businesses. Schifsky's pool page did not appear in any of the five cities. The AI Overview for Lake Elmo named Blaiser Pools (57 reviews) first. The realistic organic play is the pool landscaping angle ("landscaping around a pool", pool decks) plus a visible, linked relationship with Blaiser; competing for "pool installation" as a landscaper is not.

6. Directories take two to four slots on every hire SERP: Yelp, Houzz, Angi, HomeAdvisor, Thumbtack, BBB, Yardbook. These cannot be outranked, only joined: Houzz and Yelp profiles with reviews and project photos are the off-site work.

7. People Also Ask is size-driven. Patio PAA on every city: cost of a 20x20, 300 sq ft, 400 sq ft, 12x16, 10x10, 1000 sq ft patio, and "is it cheaper to lay concrete or pavers". Wall PAA: the 1/3 rule, whether a 4 ft wall needs drainage, cost of a 20 ft / 40 ft / 100 ft wall. Pool PAA: can I get an inground pool for $20,000 or $30,000, cost of a 12x24 pool. These are answerable from approved figures by arithmetic and are missing from the guides.

## What to change first, in order

1. Google Business Profile, Lake Elmo: add or make primary the "Landscaper" category (keep "Landscape designer" as secondary), confirm the service area includes Woodbury, Stillwater and the east metro, add Woodbury project photos and a weekly post, and run the review routine. The Woodbury pack is reachable (Pioneer, Lake Elmo, is in it). Owner action; largest lead effect of anything in this document.
2. Shakopee profile: 13 reviews, "Landscape designer", paid pack only. Point its website link at a Shakopee location page on the site (none exists), add the Shakopee and Chaska projects as photos, and get it to 40 or more reviews before expecting Chaska or Victoria packs. Owner action plus one new page (decision below).
3. City hubs: put the city in the five service headings ("Paver Patios and Hardscape in Woodbury", "Pools and Complete Pool Backyards in Woodbury") and in the first sentence of each section, so the hub competes for service-plus-city queries; add an anchor link from each service page's city list to the matching hub section. On-page, no new URLs.
4. Service pages: add a short "Where we build this" paragraph naming the priority cities with links to the hub anchors, and move the pergola page ahead of the hubs for pergola queries by naming the five cities on the pergola page. On-page.
5. Guides: add size-based cost tables (patio 10x10, 12x16, 20x20, 300, 400, 1,000 sq ft; wall 20, 40, 100 ft at 3 ft and 4 ft tall) computed from approved per-square-foot figures, and the PAA questions as FAQ entries. On-page.
6. Pools: a "landscaping around your pool" section linked from Blaiser Pools to the pool landscaping page (off-site, owner), and stop targeting "pool installation {city}" in copy.
7. Decisions: a Minnetrista hub page (justified: Maple Plain project five miles away, Lake Minnetonka lake-home intent, service prioritized by the owner); a Shakopee location page (justified by the second profile); a Woodbury pool backyard page (justified by four completed Woodbury pool backyards and no page targeting "pool Woodbury" intent that Schifsky can honestly serve). No other city-service pages: evidence is not there yet.
