What Does a Full Backyard Landscape Project Cost in the Twin Cities? [2026 Data]

A full backyard landscape project in the Twin Cities typically ranges from about $45,000 for a focused makeover to $300,000 and up for a luxury estate, with most complete transformations landing between $100,000 and $300,000. The number comes down to scope, how much hardscape, outdoor living, planting, and whether a pool is involved.

Here is how it breaks down, with real examples from projects we have built across the metro.

Backyard project cost at a glance

  • Most complete transformations land $100,000 to $300,000.
  • A focused refresh starts around $45,000 to $100,000.
  • Luxury estate projects run $300,000 and up (our largest: $689K in Lake Elmo).
  • Hardscape (patios, walls, steps) is usually the single largest line item.
  • Adding a pool is the biggest jump, moving a project well into six figures.

What drives the cost

  1. Hardscape. Paver and natural-stone patios, walkways, steps, seat walls, and retaining walls are usually the largest single line item. Proper base preparation for Minnesota’s freeze-thaw is built into that cost.
  2. A pool. Adding an inground pool is the biggest jump in budget. A pool plus the landscape around it typically moves a project well into the six figures.
  3. Outdoor living features. Outdoor kitchens, fireplaces, fire pits, pergolas, and lighting each add to the total and to how much you use the space.
  4. The site itself. Grading, drainage, access, and how much of the existing yard has to be reworked all affect the number.
  5. Plantings and finish. Trees, beds, sod or seed, and the finishing details that make a new build feel finished.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a full backyard landscape project cost in the Twin Cities? +
Most full backyard projects fall between $100,000 and $300,000, with focused makeovers starting around $45,000 and luxury estate projects running $300,000 and up. The final number depends on scope: hardscape, outdoor living features, a pool, and the size and condition of your yard.
What is the most affordable way to redo a backyard? +
A focused refresh, in the $45,000 to $100,000 range, gets you a new paver patio, grading and plantings, and one or two features. It is the best way to dramatically improve a yard without a full transformation budget.
Why do landscape projects vary so much in price? +
Scope drives almost everything. A patio refresh is very different from a complete transformation with a pool, outdoor kitchen, retaining walls, and full plantings. Site conditions, grading, drainage, and access also affect the total.
Is there a design fee, and is it credited toward the project? +
Yes. We charge a design fee because a project succeeds or fails in the design phase, so we invest real time getting it right. That fee is credited toward your installation when you move forward with us.
Do you offer financing for landscape projects? +
