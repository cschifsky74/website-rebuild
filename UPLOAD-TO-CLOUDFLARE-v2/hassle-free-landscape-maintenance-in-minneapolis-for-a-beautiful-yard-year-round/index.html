Hassle-Free Landscape Maintenance in Minneapolis for a Beautiful Yard Year-Round

Lawn Care That Builds Resilience

Schifsky Companies keeps Minneapolis yards healthy and appealing through every season. Offering expert maintenance in lawn care, bed upkeep, pruning, seasonal cleanup, snow removal, and irrigation adjustments, our team works proactively to protect plant health and spring your landscape into shape. With over 30 years of experience, we balance beautiful design with functional care for lasting results.Healthy turf begins with precise mowing and targeted feeding. We maintain optimal cutting height, leaving grass at levels that support deep roots and resist weeds. Our team applies fertilizer early in spring, overseeds bare spots, and aerates to improve nutrient flow. Regular mow-and-blow services and edge trimming keep grass from encroaching on beds and paths, maintaining crisp lines and a polished look.

Mulched Beds and Pruning for Plant Health

Clean beds protect plant roots and create visual contrast. We refresh mulch annually to suppress weeds, retain moisture, and insulate seasonal shifts. Our pruning team shapes shrubs, renews flowering branches, and removes deadwood to promote new growth. Clear beds and properly trimmed plants strengthen garden structure and reduce disease risk.

Spring and Fall Cleanups for Seasonal Transitions

Preparing for cold weather is as important as spring prep. In spring, we clear debris, clean flowerbeds, and rebuild mulch. When autumn arrives, we trim perennials, remove spent foliage, and guard young trees. Timely cleanup prevents disease spread and readies your yard for snow, ensuring healthier rebirth in spring. Proper fall preparation also reduces the risk of pests taking shelter in your yard during the winter months.

Irrigation Management That Saves Water

Efficient watering keeps plants thriving while lowering bills. We inspect irrigation systems, adjust heads for coverage, and reset schedules based on seasonal rainfall. System testing ensures no leaks or underperforming zones earlier than needed. Smart irrigation keeps moisture consistent and supports sustainable landscape practices. Regular adjustments also help minimize water waste, contributing to environmental conservation.

Snow Removal and Winter Yard Protection

Snow and ice can damage landscapes if left unchecked. Our winter care clears driveways, walkways, and patios promptly to prevent ice buildup. We apply safe deicing treatments to protect plants and hardscapes. Early snow removal also prevents damage from freeze-thaw cycles, keeping your yard functional during winter.

Native Plant Selection for Low-Maintenance Beauty

Select plants that thrive in Minnesota’s climate and curb upkeep demand. We install native shrubs, perennials, and ornamental grasses that resist pests and drought. Proper placement and soil matching help these plants grow strong and minimize long-term care. Landscapes chosen from hardy varieties reward you with color and resilience with minimal effort. These plants require less water and fewer chemical treatments, making them ideal for a sustainable and eco-friendly landscape.

Pest and Disease Control That Works Proactively

Early detection saves plants and prevents costly treatments. Our team inspects foliage and soil, identifying signs of insects or infection before outbreaks spread. We apply targeted, low-impact solutions using organic or Eco-certified treatments. Proactive control keeps green spaces healthy without unnecessary harsh chemicals. By monitoring for early signs of trouble, we help ensure that your landscape remains vibrant and disease-free throughout the year.

Communication and Customized Service Schedules

Strong service plans begin with clear communication. Our team works directly with you to determine timing, frequency, and areas to focus on. We build a plan suited to your home’s size, style, and use patterns. Consistent scheduling ensures care aligns with growing seasons and busy outdoor life.

Working With Local Expertise and Experience

Schifsky Companies serves Minneapolis, Wayzata, Woodbury, and across the metro with a team that knows local soil, climate, and plant varieties. With three decades of service and licensed crews, we deliver professional maintenance that balances aesthetics with sustainability. Our hands-on experience ensures your landscape thrives across seasons. By working closely with local suppliers and staying updated on regional trends, we ensure that every design and maintenance plan is customized to maximize success in the unique Minnesota environment.

Invest in a Greener, Stronger Yard

Routine maintenance eliminates small issues before they worsen. With consistent care from Schifsky, you gain more than curb appeal: deeper plant roots, healthier soil, fewer pest challenges, and reliable outdoor spaces. Invest in smart upkeep now to protect your landscape investment and enjoy long-term value and enjoyment.

Schedule Your Year-Round Care Today

Don’t wait for weeds to take over or winter lanes to become treacherous. Contact Schifsky Companies to plan maintenance customized to your yard’s needs. Expect professional mowing, pruning, irrigation checks, pest inspections, snow clearing, and more throughout the year.
