Built by One Family Since 1989

Founded in 1989 in Stillwater, MN, Schifsky Companies has grown from retaining walls and paver patios into a full landscape design and build company serving the Twin Cities metro. We handle design, hardscape, planting, and construction with one accountable team — and we’ve stayed family-owned the whole way.
