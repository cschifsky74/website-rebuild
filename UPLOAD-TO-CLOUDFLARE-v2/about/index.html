Our Story

A Family Business, Built on Doing It Right

Family-owned landscape design & build, serving the Twin Cities for 37+ years.

Since 1989Family-Owned

Founded in 1989 in Stillwater, MN, Schifsky Companies began with a focus on retaining walls and paver patios. Over 37+ years we’ve grown and evolved - expanding into 3D landscape design, complete design-build services, and both residential and commercial excavation.

As a proud family-owned business, we’ve stayed committed to the same core values the whole way: leadership, integrity, fortitude, and excellence. Today, Schifsky Companies is a respected name across the Twin Cities, delivering projects that are built to last.

How We Work

Design First, in 3D

We believe a project succeeds or fails in the design phase. Our designers create a tailored plan with realistic 3D renderings, so you can see and refine your outdoor space before any work begins - no guesswork. From there, one accountable team handles the build start to finish, keeping your project on schedule and clearly communicated.
