Woodbury Pool Backyards

What We Build Around a Woodbury Pool

  • Paver or porcelain pool deck sized for loungers and a dining table
  • Grading and drainage so rain runs away from the pool, not into it
  • Code-compliant aluminum fence with self-closing, self-latching gates
  • Louvered pergola or cabana for shade
  • Outdoor kitchen, bar and fire table
  • Boulder or block walls and steps where the lot slopes
  • Low-litter plantings, lighting and lawn to the deck edge
  • HOA drawings and city permit coordination
Why It Matters

Why Woodbury Pool Backyards Get Built in One Plan

Many Woodbury neighborhoods have HOA design review, and the city, like most metro cities, sets setbacks for the pool and its equipment and reviews how much of the lot is covered by hard surface. When the pool and the landscape are designed together in 3D, the pool sits where the deck, fence and pergola want it, the drawings for the HOA and the permit come from one plan, and nothing has to be moved after the shell is in the ground. All three projects on this page were built that way.

What Affects the Cost

What a Pool Backyard Costs in Woodbury

We price by scope, not by city, and the three Woodbury projects on this page are the clearest reference points we have. A complete pool-and-backyard build runs $200,000 to $430,000 or more: the fiberglass pool itself is about $130,000 installed through Blaiser Pools, and the landscape around it typically adds $75,000 to $150,000 for the deck, fence, grading, plantings and lighting before a pergola, cabana or kitchen. Poolside on the Hill came in at $120,000 for the landscape alone; Woodbury Poolside and Life on the Fairway at $425,000 and $430,000 as complete projects with every feature. Plunge pools bring the pool side down to $85,000 to $130,000; see the plunge pool page.

  • Pool size and modelA plunge pool and a full-size rectangle with a tanning ledge sit at opposite ends of the range.
  • Deck size and materialPorcelain and large-format pavers cost more per square foot than standard pavers; deck area is the biggest landscape line.
  • GradeA sloped Woodbury lot means walls and steps behind the pool, as at Poolside on the Hill.
  • Shade and kitchenA louvered pergola, cabana, outdoor kitchen and fire table are what separate the $120,000 scope from the $425,000 one.

FAQ

Common Woodbury Pool Backyards Questions

Who installs the pool, and who builds the rest? +
The pool shell is installed by Blaiser Pools, our sister company under shared ownership. Schifsky Companies designs the whole backyard and builds everything around the pool: deck, fence, grading, walls, pergola or cabana, kitchen, plantings and lighting. You deal with one plan and one schedule, and on two of the three Woodbury projects above the complete project, pool included, was delivered that way.
What does a pool backyard cost in Woodbury? +
The three Woodbury projects on this page came in at $120,000 for the landscape and hardscape around a separately installed pool, and $425,000 and $430,000 as complete projects with the pool, pergola, kitchen and every feature. Across the Twin Cities a complete pool-and-backyard build runs $200,000 to $430,000 or more, with the landscape portion typically $75,000 to $150,000.
Does Woodbury require a fence around a pool? +
Yes. Minnesota cities require a barrier around any inground pool, typically at least 4 feet high with self-closing, self-latching gates, plus setbacks for the pool and equipment and an electrical permit. We design the fence line with the deck so the gates land where you walk, and we coordinate the permits. Our pool rules guide covers the details.
My HOA has design review. Can you handle it? +
Many Woodbury neighborhoods do. Our 3D design produces the scaled drawings and material specifications an HOA asks for, and the same plan is used for the city permit, so there is one set of drawings to approve.
Can the yard be finished the same season as the pool? +
That is how Poolside on the Hill was done: Blaiser set the pool, our crew started the porcelain deck the same day, and the family had a finished backyard that summer. Because the two companies plan the job together, the deck base is scheduled around the pool set from the start.
Can I build it in phases? +
Yes. We design the complete plan first, then build in phases that match your budget and timing, so the deck built this year already allows for the pergola and kitchen planned for next year.
How far are you from Woodbury? +
Our office and showroom are in Lake Elmo, about 15 minutes from most Woodbury neighborhoods. Consultations happen at your home or in the showroom, where you can see the paver and porcelain samples and the pool models.
