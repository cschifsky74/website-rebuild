Frequently Asked Questions

What to know before you start your landscape project with Schifsky Companies.

Is the consultation really free? +
Yes. Your initial consultation and site visit are completely free with no obligation — a chance for us to understand your goals, see your property, and have an honest conversation about budget and possibilities.
Will I see the design before construction begins? +
Absolutely. Our designers create a custom plan with realistic 3D renderings so you can see and refine your space before any work starts — no guesswork.
Is there a design fee? +
We charge a design fee because we invest significant time creating the right plan, and a project succeeds or fails in the design phase. The good news: that design fee is credited toward your installation if you move forward with Schifsky.
What size projects do you take on? +
Everything from focused outdoor upgrades to complete, large-scale backyard transformations. The best first step is a quick conversation so we can understand your goals and budget.
How long does a project take? +
Timelines depend on scope, materials, and permitting. After your consultation we’ll give you a realistic schedule, and your dedicated project leader keeps you updated throughout the build.
Do you offer snow removal in winter? +
Yes — we provide reliable residential and commercial snow removal and de-icing across the Twin Cities all season long, plus year-round landscape maintenance.
What areas do you serve? +
We’re based in Lake Elmo, MN and serve the Twin Cities metro and surrounding suburbs, from Wayzata to Woodbury. Not sure if you’re in our area? Just ask and we’ll let you know.
