How Much Does an Inground Pool Cost in Minnesota? [2026 Data]

An inground pool in Minnesota typically costs around $130,000 for the pool itself. But a pool is only as good as the space around it, so we recommend budgeting at least $75,000 more for the surrounding landscape to get the full resort feel. That means a complete pool-and-backyard project usually lands between $200,000 and $430,000 or more, depending on scope.

Here is what goes into that number, with real examples from pools we have built across the Twin Cities.

Inground pool cost at a glance

  • The pool itself averages around $130,000 (fiberglass or vinyl).
  • We recommend at least $75,000 for the surrounding landscape and hardscape.
  • A complete pool-and-backyard project usually lands $200,000 to $430,000+.
  • We install fiberglass and vinyl pools, not concrete or gunite.
  • Real Twin Cities builds ranged from $192K (Hudson) to $430K (Woodbury).
  • Financing is available through Lyon Financial with no equity or appraisal required.
  • Best time to plan: design over winter to be first in line for a summer build.

Fiberglass vs. vinyl: how it affects cost

We install two types of pools, both through trusted Latham dealers:

  • Fiberglass (through our sister company, Blaiser Pools): a smooth, durable, low-maintenance surface that installs quickly. It comes in set shapes and sizes.
  • Vinyl-liner (through Legacy Pools): typically lower up-front cost and can be built to almost any shape and size, with the liner refreshed roughly every 10 years.

We do not install concrete or gunite pools. For a deeper look, read our guide on fiberglass vs. vinyl pools in Minnesota.

Why the landscape budget matters

The single most common pool regret is not the pool, it is everything around it: a deck that is too small, a muddy yard that never got finished, landscaping that ran out of budget. Because we are a design-build company, we plan the pool, patio, grading, plantings, and outdoor living together, and show it to you in 3D before we dig. That is why we recommend at least $75,000 for the surrounding space, so the day the water goes in, the whole backyard is finished.

What is included

A complete pool project with us covers the design, the pool (installed through Blaiser or Legacy), and everything around it: the paver patio, retaining walls and grading, any fire features or outdoor kitchen, plus plantings, lighting, and the finishing touches that make it a true backyard resort.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does an inground pool cost in Minnesota? +

Are fiberglass or vinyl pools cheaper in Minnesota? +
Vinyl-liner pools usually cost less up front and can be built to almost any shape and size, with the liner refreshed every 10 or so years. Fiberglass pools have a smooth, durable, low-maintenance surface and install quickly, but come in set shapes and sizes. We install both and help you weigh them.
Why should I budget for landscaping around the pool? +
Most pool buyers regret the parts around the pool, not the pool itself: the cramped deck, the leftover muddy yard, the unfinished landscaping. Budgeting for the patio, grading, plantings, and outdoor living gives you a finished resort-style space instead of a pool dropped into a construction site.
Who installs the pool? +
Pools are installed through Blaiser Pools (our sister company, for fiberglass) and Legacy Pools (for vinyl), both Latham dealers, while our team designs and builds everything around the pool: patio, grading, walls, plantings, and lighting, as the accountable lead on your project.
Can you finance a pool project? +
Yes. We partner with Lyon Financial for low, fixed-rate home-improvement loans with no equity or appraisals required. You can estimate a monthly payment on our financing page.
