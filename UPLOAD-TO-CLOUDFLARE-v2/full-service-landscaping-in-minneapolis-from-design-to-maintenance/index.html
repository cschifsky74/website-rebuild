Full-Service Landscaping in Minneapolis: From Design to Maintenance

The Importance of Professional Landscaping

A well-designed landscape boosts curb appeal, increases property value, and creates an inviting outdoor space. Professional landscaping keeps your yard functional, visually appealing, and easy to maintain. Expert services ensure long-term beauty and efficiency for both residential and commercial properties.

Why Work With Schifsky Companies?

Decades of Landscaping Experience

At Schifsky Companies, we provide full-service landscaping in Minneapolis. With decades of experience, we handle everything from landscape design to ongoing maintenance. Our team creates customized outdoor spaces that align with your vision while ensuring lasting durability and beauty.

Custom Landscape Design and Installation

Every great landscape starts with a thoughtful design. We work closely with you to develop a layout that enhances your property’s aesthetics and functionality. From selecting the right plants to constructing patios and retaining walls, we bring expertise and precision to every project.

Year-Round Lawn and Garden Maintenance

A healthy landscape requires ongoing care. Our services include lawn mowing, seasonal cleanups, pruning, and fertilization to keep your yard looking its best. With professional maintenance plans, your outdoor space remains vibrant in every season.

Seasonal Landscaping Services

Minneapolis experiences extreme weather, and landscapes must be prepared for every season. We provide fall cleanup, winter protection, and spring revitalization to keep plants, trees, and lawns in peak condition. Proper seasonal care extends the life of your landscape and enhances its appearance.

Enhancing Outdoor Spaces with Hardscaping

Functional and Beautiful Hardscaping

Well-designed hardscaping improves both aesthetics and functionality. We install patios, walkways, retaining walls, and outdoor living spaces using high-quality materials. These additions increase durability and make your yard more usable for entertaining and relaxation.
