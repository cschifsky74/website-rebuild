Why It Matters

Why We Design in 3D First

A beautiful yard starts on paper, not in the dirt. With full 3D renderings you can walk through your space before we break ground - adjusting layouts, comparing materials, and getting it exactly right while changes are still free. It’s the difference between hoping it turns out and knowing it will.

How We Work

Our Design-Build Process

No guesswork and no surprises - here’s exactly how your project comes together, from the first conversation to the final walkthrough.

Consultation & Site Inspection

We talk through how you want to use the space and set a clear scope, then visit your property to take precise measurements and flag any challenges. You’ll get an estimated cost up front, and a small design deposit gets us started.

Design Preview

We present the first draft of your 3D design - often over a quick video call, and walk through material samples so you can picture them on your own property. We note every change you want.

Design Approval & Quote

We bring back the revised 3D design with your selections and present a firm, itemized project quote. Once you approve and sign, we reserve your project’s start week on the schedule.

Project Commencement

Before we dig, our project management team meets you on site to paint and lay out the job. Construction starts within the agreed timeframe, with your project manager overseeing quality and keeping you updated throughout.

Project Completion

We finish with a comprehensive final walkthrough to confirm every detail meets our standard, and yours - before the project is considered complete.

FAQ

Common Landscape Design Questions

Is there a design fee? +
Yes - we charge a design fee because a project succeeds or fails in the design phase, so we invest real time getting it right. The good news: that fee is credited 100% toward your installation if you move forward with Schifsky.
Will I see my design before construction begins? +
Absolutely. We create realistic 3D renderings so you can see and refine your space before any work starts - no guesswork.
Do you handle both the design and the build? +
Yes - we’re a true design-build company. One accountable team takes your project from first sketch to final walkthrough, so nothing gets lost in handoffs between trades.
What does landscape design cost? +
It depends on the scope of your project. We give you an honest estimate range at your free consultation, then a firm, itemized quote once the design and materials are set.
How long does the design process take? +
It varies with scope, but we’ll give you a realistic timeline up front and keep you updated throughout - from design through the final walkthrough.
