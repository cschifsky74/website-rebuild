Working in Chaska

Chaska blends established neighborhoods with newer developments across rolling Carver County terrain, and we’ve worked in the area, including nearby Shakopee. We design and build patios, pools, retaining walls and complete landscapes suited to Chaska homes.

Our family-owned team has spent 37+ years building patios, pools, pergolas, outdoor kitchens, fire features, retaining walls and complete landscapes across the Twin Cities. Every Chaska project is designed in 3D first and built by one accountable crew - on schedule and clearly communicated.

Chaska is the Carver County seat, set on the Minnesota River with Chaska Creek running through town, the historic downtown, the planned Jonathan neighborhoods and newer development on rolling ground to the north and west. Lots run from small in-town yards to large hillside parcels with river-valley views.

Our closest published projects are in neighboring Shakopee, The Home Resort and The Sustainable Modern, both pool backyards on sloped, wooded lots, and they are the best preview of how we work in Chaska: solve the grade with walls and drainage, then build the pool deck, outdoor living and plantings as one plan. The Sustainable Modern

A pool and full landscape designed to complement a modern Sustainable 9 home on a wooded Shakopee lakeside lot.

About this project

The homeowners came to us a couple of years after Sustainable 9 Builders finished this striking modern home, with a backyard that was still a blank slope down to the lake. Our job was to design a landscape that lived up to the architecture and made the most of a wooded, sloped lot overlooking the water. We set a crisp rectangular fiberglass pool into a wide porcelain paver deck, then held the grade with tiered block walls that double as planters for hydrangeas and ornamental grasses. A gas fire table with lounge seating anchors one end of the pool deck, a set of wide paver steps ties the pool level to the lower lawn, and layered evergreen plantings screen the neighbors without blocking the water. Black aluminum fencing, a custom cedar gate, and low-voltage lighting finish a backyard that is modern on the surface and built for Minnesota underneath.

Hamptons in the Heartland

A pool & landscape remodel mixing natural stone and modern elements for an East Coast Hamptons feel.

About this project

This Maple Plain remodel set out to bring the timeless, breezy feel of the Hamptons to the heartland. We paired a clean rectangular pool with natural-stone coping and decking, wrapped it in boulder-edged planting beds and layered gardens, and set loungers and umbrellas along the water for easy summer living. The aerials tell the story best - a refined, resort-caliber backyard that feels like a coastal retreat, right here in Minnesota.
