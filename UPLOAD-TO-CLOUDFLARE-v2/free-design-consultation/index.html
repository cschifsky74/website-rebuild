Common Questions

What to Know Before You Start

What size projects do you take on? +
We take on a wide range of projects - from focused outdoor upgrades to complete, large-scale backyard transformations. Whatever stage you're at, the best first step is a quick conversation so we can understand your goals and budget.
Is the design consultation really free? +
Yes. Your initial consultation and site visit are completely free with no obligation - a chance for us to understand your goals, see your property, and have an honest conversation about budget and possibilities.
Are the designs free? +
No, and that's intentional. We charge a design fee because we invest significant time creating the right plan for your project, and we believe a project succeeds or fails in the design phase, so it's not something we skimp on. The good news: that design fee is credited 100% toward your installation if you choose to move forward with Schifsky.
Will I see the design before construction begins? +
Absolutely. Our designers create a custom plan with realistic 3D renderings so you can see and refine your space before any work starts - no guesswork.
How long does a project take? +
Timelines depend on scope, materials, and permitting. After your consultation we'll give you a realistic schedule, and your dedicated project leader keeps you updated throughout the build.
Are you licensed, insured, and do you offer a warranty? +
Yes - Schifsky Companies is fully licensed and insured, and we back our work with a workmanship warranty. You can invest with confidence.
What areas do you serve? +
We're based in Lake Elmo, MN and serve the Twin Cities metro and surrounding suburbs, from Wayzata to Woodbury. Not sure if you're in our area? Just ask in the form above and we'll let you know.
