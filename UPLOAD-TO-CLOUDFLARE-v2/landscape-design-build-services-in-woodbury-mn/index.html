Paver Patios and Hardscape

A Woodbury patio starts with layout: where the sun lands in the evening, how you get from the kitchen door to the grill, where the steps and grade changes fall on a sloped lot. Then material: concrete pavers for range and value, porcelain for a clean modern surface, natural stone for a path or seating ledge. Underneath all of it is a deep compacted base graded to move water away from the house, which is the part that decides whether a patio still sits flat in ten winters.

In Woodbury, The Modern Revival (above) is a hardscape-led renovation on an established lot and Woodbury Poolside shows a large-format paver deck around a pool. The photos here add a Stillwater patio and stoop renovation and a Maple Plain pool deck.

Pools and Complete Pool Backyards

Three of the four Woodbury projects on this page are pool backyards, and each was designed as one plan: the pool, the deck around it, the pergola or cabana for shade, the kitchen, the grading and the plantings. That is the difference between a pool and a pool backyard, and it is why we recommend budgeting for the space around the pool from the start.

Fiberglass shells are installed through our sister company Blaiser Pools; our team designs and builds everything around them. The three models we install most often are on the pool page.

Outdoor Kitchens, Fire Features and Pergolas

All three Woodbury projects on this page have a fire feature, two have a built-in outdoor kitchen and bar, and two have a louvered aluminum pergola. The pattern is not a coincidence: a Woodbury backyard gets used in the evening, in the shoulder seasons, and, on open lots like Woodbury Poolside, when the wind picks up, so shade, heat and a place to cook are what turn a patio into a room.

Louvered pergolas, zip screens, infrared heaters and lighting are planned into the hardscape so the gas, electrical and footings go in once.

Retaining Walls and Grading

On a sloped Woodbury lot, walls do two jobs: hold the grade so a patio or pool deck can be level, and carve out planting terraces that soften a tall foundation. The Modern Revival used block walls for both, and the Birchwood Village and Dellwood photos here show the natural stone and boulder options.

Walls holding a driveway, structure or slope above them can need engineering at lower heights than you would expect, and the requirement varies by city. We check it for your lot during the assessment, and we handle the drainage behind the wall that keeps it standing straight through freeze-thaw.

Woodbury Landscaping FAQ

Common Questions from Woodbury Homeowners

Do you serve all of Woodbury? +
Yes. Our office and showroom are in Lake Elmo, about 15 minutes from most Woodbury neighborhoods, and we design and build throughout the city, from established lots to new construction.
What does a backyard project in Woodbury cost? +
We price by scope, not by city. A focused paver patio typically runs $15,000 to $30,000, a complete backyard transformation $100,000 to $300,000, and a pool plus complete backyard $200,000 to $430,000 or more. The Woodbury projects on this page came in at $235,000, $425,000 and $430,000 as complete projects, and $120,000 for the landscape and hardscape around a pool installed separately. See the Woodbury paver patio cost guide for the patio breakdown.
Can a Woodbury backyard be built in phases? +
Yes. We design the complete plan first, then build it in phases that match your budget and timing, so the pool deck poured this year already allows for the pergola and kitchen planned for next year. One of the reviews on this page describes a project built that way.
Do you handle HOA and city approvals? +
Many Woodbury neighborhoods have HOA design review. Our 3D design produces the scaled drawings and material specifications an HOA asks for, and we coordinate the engineering and permits required for pools, retaining walls and structures.
When should I start, and how long does it take? +
Design in fall or winter for a spring start; the showroom is open year-round by appointment. Construction time depends on scope, from a couple of weeks for a patio to a season for a full pool backyard, and we give you a realistic timeline up front and keep you updated through the final walkthrough.
Can you work around an existing deck, pool or mature trees? +
Yes. The Modern Revival was a renovation of an established yard with mature trees around it. We start every design from what is already there and worth keeping, and our project management team lays out the job with you on site before we dig.
Do you offer 3D design, and are you licensed and insured? +
Yes to both. Every project starts with realistic 3D design so you can see it before we build, and Schifsky Companies is fully licensed and insured, family-owned since 1989.
