How Much Does a Paver Patio Cost in Lake Elmo, MN? [2026 Data]

A professionally installed paver patio in Lake Elmo, MN typically costs $45 to $62 per square foot for concrete pavers, which works out to roughly $15,000 to $30,000 for a backyard patio. Porcelain and natural stone add another $10 to $15 per square foot. Schifsky Companies is headquartered right here in Lake Elmo, so here is the local 2026 breakdown.

Building on Lake Elmo lots

Lake Elmo is different from a dense inner-ring suburb. Many properties sit on larger, semi-rural lots, sometimes with wells, septic systems, and mature trees to work around, and some homes are newer construction on graded but unfinished ground. That affects patio work: excavation, drainage, and grading have to account for the full site, not just the patio footprint. Because we are based in Lake Elmo, we know the local soils and the city’s setback and drainage requirements, and we handle any permitting for you.

Why the base matters in Lake Elmo’s climate

Most failed patios here do not fail because of the pavers, they fail because of what is underneath. Lake Elmo’s freeze-thaw cycles heave anything that was not built on a properly compacted, well-drained base. That is why we over-build the part you never see, so the part you do see stays flat and tight for decades. If a paver ever settles, it can be lifted and reset, something you cannot do with poured concrete.

How much does a paver patio cost in Lake Elmo, MN? +

Do you need a permit for a patio in Lake Elmo? +
A ground-level, freestanding paver patio in Lake Elmo generally does not require a building permit, though the city has setback and drainage requirements, and many properties sit on larger lots with septic and well considerations. Attached, raised, or roofed structures do require permits. We are based in Lake Elmo and handle all of this for you.
Are you local to Lake Elmo? +

Do paver patios hold up in Lake Elmo winters? +
Yes, when built on the right base. Lake Elmo's freeze-thaw cycles heave anything set on a poor base, so we build on a deep, compacted, well-drained foundation. Interlocking pavers flex with that movement, and any settled paver can be lifted and reset, unlike a poured slab that cracks.
When is the best time to install a patio in Lake Elmo? +
Minnesota's build season runs roughly late April through October, and spring fills up fastest. Homeowners who plan over the winter and book by February get the best scheduling for a spring or summer build.
