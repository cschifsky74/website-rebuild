FAQ

Common Snow & Ice Removal Questions

Do you handle both residential and commercial snow removal? +
Yes. We clear residential driveways and walkways and manage commercial lots, entrances, and sidewalks, with priority service for properties where access and safety can’t wait.
At what snowfall do you come out? +
For subscription clients we service at a set trigger depth (commonly around two inches) and monitor storms so you don’t have to call. We’ll set the trigger and expectations with you when you sign up.
Is salting or de-icing included? +
De-icing and salting are available as part of your service plan. We’ll recommend the right level of ice control for your property and traffic so surfaces stay safe between snowfalls.
Is it a seasonal contract or per-visit? +
We offer a subscription service for consistent, priority care all winter, and can also handle as-needed visits. Most clients prefer the subscription so they’re covered automatically when storms hit.
