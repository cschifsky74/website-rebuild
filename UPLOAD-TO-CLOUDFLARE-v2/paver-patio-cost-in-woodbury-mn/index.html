How Much Does a Paver Patio Cost in Woodbury, MN? [2026 Data]

A professionally installed paver patio in Woodbury, MN typically costs $45 to $62 per square foot for concrete pavers, which works out to roughly $15,000 to $30,000 for a backyard patio. Porcelain and natural stone add another $10 to $15 per square foot. We are based just minutes away in Lake Elmo and build throughout Woodbury, so here is the local 2026 breakdown.

Woodbury paver patio cost at a glance

  • A typical Woodbury paver patio runs $15,000 to $30,000 installed.
  • Concrete pavers cost $45 to $62 per square foot, fully installed.
  • Porcelain and natural stone add $10 to $15 per square foot.
  • A mid-size 350 sq ft patio averages around $18,500 before add-ons.
  • Ground-level patios usually need no permit, but check HOA and setback rules.
  • Add-ons (seat walls, fire pit, outdoor kitchen, lighting) add $3,000 to $20,000+.
  • We build across Prestwick, Stonemill Farms, Dancing Waters, and Eagle Valley.

Permits and HOAs in Woodbury

In most cases a ground-level, freestanding paver patio in Woodbury does not require a building permit. Where it gets local: many Woodbury neighborhoods, especially newer developments like Stonemill Farms, Dancing Waters, and Prestwick, have HOA architectural review for exterior projects, and the city enforces setback and impervious-surface rules. Attached, raised, or roofed structures do require permits. We confirm all of this with the City of Woodbury and your HOA before we build, so nothing stalls mid-project.

Why the base matters in Woodbury’s climate

Most failed patios here do not fail because of the pavers, they fail because of what is underneath. Woodbury’s freeze-thaw cycles heave anything that was not built on a properly compacted, well-drained base. That is why we over-build the part you never see, so the part you do see stays flat and tight for decades. If a paver ever settles, it can be lifted and reset, something you cannot do with poured concrete.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a paver patio cost in Woodbury, MN? +
A professionally installed paver patio in Woodbury generally runs $45 to $62 per square foot, or roughly $15,000 to $30,000 for a typical backyard patio. Porcelain and natural stone add $10 to $15 per square foot. Larger patios with seat walls, fire features, or an outdoor kitchen run higher.
Do you need a permit for a patio in Woodbury? +
A ground-level, freestanding paver patio in Woodbury generally does not require a building permit, but the city has setback and drainage rules, and some neighborhoods have HOA design review. Attached, raised, or roofed structures do require permits. We confirm the requirements with the City of Woodbury and handle permitting as part of the project.
Which Woodbury neighborhoods do you build in? +
We build throughout Woodbury, from established areas like Eagle Valley to newer developments like Stonemill Farms, Dancing Waters, and Prestwick. We are based just minutes away in Lake Elmo.
Do paver patios hold up in Woodbury winters? +
Yes, when built on the right base. Woodbury's freeze-thaw cycles heave anything set on a poor base, so we build on a deep, compacted, well-drained foundation. Interlocking pavers flex with that movement, and any settled paver can be lifted and reset, unlike a poured slab that cracks.
How long does a paver patio take to build in Woodbury? +
Most residential paver patios take a few days to a couple of weeks depending on size, access, and features. Minnesota's build season runs roughly late April through October, and spring books up fast, so we recommend planning over the winter.
