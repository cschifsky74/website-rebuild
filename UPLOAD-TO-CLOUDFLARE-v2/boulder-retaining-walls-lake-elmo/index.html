Why It Matters

More Than Looks - Protection for Your Property

A slope that washes out or a yard that sheds water toward your foundation is an expensive problem waiting to happen. A properly engineered boulder wall holds the grade, redirects water, stops erosion, and turns a steep, unusable bank into level, usable yard - all while adding lasting value and natural beauty. Done right, it’s one of the few features that’s as functional as it is gorgeous.

FAQ

Common Boulder Retaining Walls Questions

How tall can a retaining wall be before it needs engineering or a permit? +
It varies by city, but walls over roughly four feet (or walls holding a heavy load above them) typically need engineering and a permit. We handle that assessment for you and build to code so the wall is safe and approved.
Boulder wall vs. a modular block wall, which should I choose? +
Boulders give a natural, organic, “always been here” look and handle irregular slopes beautifully. Block walls offer crisp, uniform lines. Both can be engineered to last - it largely comes down to the look you want and the site.
Why does a retaining wall need drainage behind it? +
Water trapped behind a wall creates enormous pressure and is the number-one reason walls fail or lean. We build gravel backfill and drainage in behind every wall so water escapes instead of pushing the wall out.
Do you do lakeshore riprap and shoreline protection? +
Yes. We install riprap and natural-stone shoreline protection to stop erosion and stabilize banks, designed to handle wave action and ice while looking like it belongs.
