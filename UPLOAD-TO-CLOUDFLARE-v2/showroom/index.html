Open Year-Round

Don’t Wait for Spring to Start Dreaming

Our showroom is open all year by appointment, and winter is actually the smartest time to visit. A photo on a screen cannot tell you how a paver reads in the light, how natural stone feels underfoot, or how porcelain holds its color through a Minnesota winter. Here you can see and feel the real thing, browse the full lineup of pool models, and work through your design with a designer, all in a warm room while your yard sits under snow.

Planning over the winter pays off: the homeowners who design in the off-season are the ones who break ground first when the ground thaws, instead of waiting in the spring rush. Come get inspired now, and be ready to build the moment the season opens.
