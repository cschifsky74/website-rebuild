How Much Does a Paver Patio Cost in White Bear Lake, MN? [2026 Data]

A professionally installed paver patio in the White Bear Lake area typically costs $45 to $62 per square foot for concrete pavers, which works out to roughly $15,000 to $30,000 for a backyard patio. Porcelain and natural stone add another $10 to $15 per square foot. Lakefront and near-lake lots bring shoreland rules and grade into the picture, so here is the local 2026 breakdown.

White Bear Lake paver patio cost at a glance

  • A typical White Bear Lake paver patio runs $15,000 to $30,000 installed.
  • Concrete pavers cost $45 to $62 per square foot, fully installed.
  • Porcelain and natural stone add $10 to $15 per square foot.
  • A mid-size 350 sq ft patio averages around $18,500 before add-ons.
  • Lakefront lots often need terracing, retaining walls, and shoreland-compliant drainage.
  • Shoreland rules govern setbacks from the water and impervious-surface limits.
  • Add-ons (seat walls, fire pit, outdoor kitchen, lighting) add $3,000 to $20,000+.

Building on White Bear Lake shoreland

Near the lake, the rules change. Shoreland regulations govern how close to the water you can build, how much impervious surface a lot can have, and how drainage must be managed to protect water quality. On a lakefront lot, patio work is rarely just a patio, it usually involves grading, terracing, and retaining walls to hold a slope and keep runoff in check. We know these requirements, coordinate with your city and any shoreland review, and design the whole space to be both compliant and beautiful.

Why the base matters in this climate

Most failed patios here do not fail because of the pavers, they fail because of what is underneath. The area’s freeze-thaw cycles heave anything that was not built on a properly compacted, well-drained base, and lakefront grade raises the stakes. That is why we over-build the part you never see, so the part you do see stays flat and tight for decades. If a paver ever settles, it can be lifted and reset, something you cannot do with poured concrete.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a paver patio cost in White Bear Lake, MN? +
A professionally installed paver patio in the White Bear Lake area generally runs $45 to $62 per square foot, or roughly $15,000 to $30,000 for a typical backyard patio. Porcelain and natural stone add $10 to $15 per square foot. Lakefront lots that need terracing, retaining walls, or shoreland-compliant drainage run higher.
Do you need a permit for a patio near White Bear Lake? +
A ground-level, freestanding paver patio usually does not require a building permit, but lakefront and near-lake lots fall under shoreland rules that govern setbacks from the water, impervious-surface limits, and drainage. These matter a lot near White Bear Lake. We confirm the requirements with your city and any shoreland regulations and handle permitting for you.
Can you build a patio on a steep lakefront lot? +
Yes. Steep shoreline lots are some of our favorite projects. On White Bear Lake we terraced a steep Birchwood Village lot into usable, level lawn and garden space with tiered natural-stone retaining walls, cable railings, and stairs down to the water. Terracing adds cost through engineering and wall work, but it turns an unusable slope into premium lakefront living.
Do paver patios hold up in White Bear Lake winters? +
Yes, when built on the right base. The area's freeze-thaw cycles heave anything set on a poor base, so we build on a deep, compacted, well-drained foundation. Interlocking pavers flex with that movement, and any settled paver can be lifted and reset, unlike a poured slab that cracks.
When is the best time to install a patio near White Bear Lake? +
Minnesota's build season runs roughly late April through October, and spring fills up fastest. Homeowners who plan over the winter and book by February get the best scheduling for a spring or summer build.
