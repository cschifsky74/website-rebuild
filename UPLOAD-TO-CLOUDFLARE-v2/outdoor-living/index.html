Outdoor Living Spaces & Kitchens

We design outdoor living spaces made for gathering - built-in kitchens, fireplaces, fire pits, and comfortable seating areas that extend your season and your square footage.

Outdoor living works best when every element is planned together. As a design-build company, we integrate your kitchen, fire feature, patio, and landscape into one cohesive space - designed in 3D first, then built by one accountable team.

FAQ

Common Outdoor Living Questions

Can I really use an outdoor living space in Minnesota? +
Yes - that’s exactly what we design for. A fireplace or fire pit, the right overhead cover, and built-in heaters extend your usable season well past summer. Many clients use these spaces from early spring into late fall, and on milder winter days.
Gas or wood-burning fire feature? +
Gas is instant, clean, and easy - flip a switch and you’re done. Wood-burning brings the crackle, smell, and bigger heat that some people love. We install both and help you weigh convenience against ambiance.
Built-in grill or freestanding? +
A built-in grill and counter looks seamless and adds prep and storage space, while a freestanding setup costs less and can move. We’ll design around the cooking setup you want.
Do you integrate the kitchen and fire features with the patio and landscape? +
Always. As a design-build company we plan the whole space together so the kitchen, fire feature, patio, lighting, and plantings work as one - no awkward handoffs between trades.
